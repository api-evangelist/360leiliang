# 360leiliang (辅布司 / Fubusi)

> 360leiliang is a Chinese S2B business-to-business e-commerce platform for textile trim and
> accessories — lace, embroidery and knitted narrow fabrics in nylon, polyester, rayon and cotton —
> sold by the yard to garment manufacturers. The platform and its companion mobile app are owned and
> operated by 福建东南西北网络科技有限公司 (Fujian Dongnanxibei Network Technology Co., Ltd.) of Fuzhou,
> Fujian, China. Founded 2014.
